Plan your visit

Visiting a new church can feel like a big step. Here is what to expect when you come.

Where we meet

Via Tre Venezie 20, Padova, Italy

When you arrive, an usher will welcome you, help you find a seat and show you where Children’s Church meets.

Get directions
What happens in a service?

We begin with lively praise and worship, followed by a message from the Bible. At the end there is time for prayer, and anyone who would like prayer can come forward.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. Some people dress up for church and others come casual, and you will be welcome either way.

Is there something for my children?

Yes. Children’s Church runs during the Sunday service for ages 3 to 12, with Bible stories, songs and crafts. Babies and toddlers are welcome to stay with their parents.

Which language are services in?

Services are held in English. Our members come from many countries, so if another language would help you, tell an usher and we will do our best.

Can I watch online?

Yes. Sunday services are streamed live, and recent messages are listed further down this page.

Who we are

Christ Apostolic Church of God Mission (C.A.C.G.M.) is a Bible-believing, Spirit-filled church.

Each part of our name says something about who we are. Christ comes first, because Jesus is the head of the church and the centre of everything we do (Colossians 1:18). We are apostolic, which means we hold to the teaching of the apostles and expect the same power of the Holy Spirit that they knew (Acts 2:42).

And we are a mission. God sends us to carry the good news of Jesus to our neighbours, our city and the nations, and every member has a part to play (Mark 16:15).

  • Worship

    We worship God in spirit and in truth, with joy and reverence.

    John 4:24

  • The Word

    We build our lives, our families and our decisions on the Bible.

    2 Timothy 3:16

  • Prayer

    We pray with faith, together and often, and we expect God to answer.

    James 5:16

  • The nations

    We carry the good news of Jesus Christ to every nation.

    Matthew 28:19

A word from our pastor

Welcome, and thank you for visiting. Whether you have followed Jesus for many years or you are only beginning to ask questions, there is a place for you in this family.

Jesus promised that when the Holy Spirit comes, ordinary people receive power to live for God and to serve others (Acts 1:8). Our prayer is that everyone who joins us meets God personally and goes home strengthened.

Come and worship with us this Sunday. Bring your family, bring your questions and bring your expectation.

God bless you,Senior Pastor

Ministries

Every member is a minister. These are some of the ways you can serve, grow and belong.

  • Children’s Church

    Bible stories, songs and crafts for ages 3 to 12 during the Sunday service.

  • Youth and Young Adults

    Fellowship, mentoring and honest conversations about faith for ages 13 to 30.

  • Women’s Fellowship

    Prayer, Bible study and friendship for women in every season of life.

  • Men’s Fellowship

    Men growing together as sons, husbands, fathers and leaders.

  • Worship and Choir

    Singers and musicians who lead the church in praise every week.

  • Prayer and Intercession

    The team that prays for the church, our city and the nations.

  • Evangelism and Missions

    Outreach in our neighbourhood, hospital visits and support for missions abroad.

  • Ushering and Hospitality

    The first faces you meet on Sunday, helping everyone feel at home.

  • Media and Technical

    Sound, cameras, livestream and graphics. New volunteers are trained.
